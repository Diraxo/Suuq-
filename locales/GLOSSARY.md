## Excluded — never translate (see CLAUDE.md §15/§39)
Language names (English/Somali/አማርኛ), currency codes (USD/ETB/EUR), country
names, "Google", "Suuq+", "Cloudflare"/"R2", user-generated content (listing
titles/descriptions, chat messages, bios, reviews), emails, usernames, URLs,
IDs/reference numbers, and any internal enum/status/route value.
